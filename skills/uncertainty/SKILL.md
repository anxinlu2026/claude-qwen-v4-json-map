# uncertainty — goes with every work order

You may reason and compute as you need. A conclusion counts only after a tool has confirmed it.

If you catch yourself writing "let me reconsider", "actually, hold on", "let me double-check" or
"one final time" for the second time on the same question, you cannot settle it by yourself.
Stop there and send:

    rtl ask "unsure about: ... / because: ... / the readings I cannot choose between: A ..., B ... / what would settle it: ..."

Then wait. Do not think further on it.
The same holds when the work order's time is used up: the tool then (besides `rtl assumption`, which stays open to record your conclusions)
accepts only `rtl ask` and `rtl next`.
A report does not have to be a conclusion: data plus an open question is a complete report.
